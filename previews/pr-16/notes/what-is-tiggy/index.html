What is an OpenClaw agent? Introducing Tiggy

Project period · February–September 2026

A practical AI assistant, a long-running home technology project, and something closer to a digital familiar than a chatbot.

In brief

  • Tiggy is Phil’s personal AI assistant: practical, slightly tigerish and resident on a Mac mini.
  • OpenClaw connects replaceable language models to tools, memory, messaging and home systems.
  • Identity and continuity live outside any one model in durable notes, rules and project handoffs.

Tiggy is a personal OpenClaw agent: an AI assistant running on a Mac mini, connected to tools, durable project memory and messaging rather than confined to one chat window or one language model. “Tiggy” is the name of this installation and continuing working identity; OpenClaw is the agent platform underneath it.

Revision context: the setup described here evolved between February and September 2026. This article was rechecked against OpenClaw 2026.9.4 on macOS; individual experiments elsewhere in the notebook retain their own project periods.

More than a model

“Assistant” describes the job. Digital familiar better describes the continuing character that knows the workshop, remembers decisions and works alongside one person over time.

OpenClaw connects the parts:

  • language models for reasoning and writing;
  • tools for files, code, browsers, messaging and home systems;
  • durable Markdown memory and project handoffs;
  • rules around private information and external actions;
  • Telegram and experimental voice interfaces.

Models can change with cost, speed and capability. The identity and working context sit outside them, so an upgrade need not mean meeting a stranger.

Deliberate continuity

Phil named the original Tiggy on 14 February 2026, her first night online. She began on an Ubuntu virtual machine; a later Tiggy2 continued the experiment; their useful context eventually moved into the current Mac mini installation.

The reusable lesson is simple: decisions, preferences and failures must be written down rather than trapped inside an expiring chat window.

Work and boundaries

Tiggy investigates home infrastructure, writes and tests scripts, designs automations, researches local AI, runs experiments and maintains project handoffs. The operating rule is be genuinely helpful, not performatively helpful: evidence before confident claims, concise answers for simple problems and technical depth when needed.

Internal inspection and reversible work can be proactive. Private information stays private; messages, public posts and consequential changes need clear authority. Completion is verified through the real system, not inferred from a plausible-looking command.

Why the notebook exists

Raw chats and terminal logs are poor explanations. The notebook extracts the question, experiment, evidence, result and remaining uncertainty.

Tiggy drafts in a private pull request. Phil reviews the rendered page on his phone. His merge publishes only the generated site. The notebook is therefore both documentation and another Tiggy experiment: a small machine for turning shared work into durable, reviewed knowledge.
