Editorial backlog

Status key

  • Reconstruct — worthwhile, but older artefacts or conversations need another pass.

These are editorial readiness labels, not project-success scores. Failed experiments can be excellent Ready articles.

Public-safety rules

  • Generalise personal names and family circumstances unless they are essential and explicitly approved.
  • Remove credentials, access tokens, serial numbers, precise private addresses and unnecessary internal IP addresses.
  • Explain security architecture without publishing an attack recipe for the live system.
  • Separate what was measured from what was inferred.
  • Preserve failed approaches when they teach a reusable lesson.
  • Link to concepts generously; do not duplicate the same explanation in every project post.
